# garisenda

Ground movements from tunneling and their effect on utilities and buildings: greenfield closed-form and empirical methods, made traceable. Every result records its inputs, its method, its source and the limits it was checked against, comes with a figure, and says when it falls outside a method's range, which is where a numerical model is needed.

The name is the Garisenda, the leaning tower in Bologna whose lean comes from settlement of its foundations.

> **Status:** in design; nothing is built yet. The plan is in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).
